01Standards watch

Trends & standards watch

Twelve videos on where field connectivity is actually heading. The short version: Ethernet-APL standardised the wire, not the protocol. HART-IP, PROFINET, EtherNet/IP and OPC UA FX now share one two-wire cable to the same devices. FOUNDATION Fieldbus did not lose to a rival protocol — it lost to the layer underneath it.

Video library · 12 videos Open the Trends & standards watch section Ethernet-APL, HART-IP, OPC UA FX, NOA, MTP, OT security, digital twin Go to the videos →
  • Ethernet-APL10BASE-T1L over two wires: 10 Mbit/s, 1 km trunk, power on the same pair, Ex zone 0/1/2 with 2-WISE. It replaces the physical layer, nothing above it.
  • HART-IPThe cheapest migration for most plants: the same HART devices and workflows, Ethernet transport instead of a 4–20 mA loop.
  • OPC UA FXController-to-controller exchange with a standard data model. The multi-vendor bench demos matter more than the specification text.
  • NOA and MTPThe second channel for monitoring without touching the control network, and modular skids that integrate without custom engineering.
  • OT securityIEC 62443 has moved from a project afterthought to a procurement gate. Segmentation vocabulary is now expected of instrument engineers.
  • Digital twinUseful where the model is honest about its steady-state assumptions, and quietly wrong where it is not.
