01Programs at a glance

Two routes into the same trade

BCIT trains instrumentation technicians to install, calibrate and maintain field instruments and PLCs. HUST's EE-E8 trains control and automation engineers across control theory, electronics, drives and intelligent systems. Both converge on the loops, signals and standards this guide covers.

BCIT — Industrial Instrumentation & Process Control Technician

Diploma · full-time · in person · School of Energy, Canada

Half hands-on; field instruments, PLCs, calibration and tuning.

HUST EE-E8 — Control Engineering & Automation

Advanced program · 4 years · 132 credits · ASIIN accredited, Vietnam

English-taught; control theory, electronics, drives, robotics, AI.

02BCIT — overview

A trade, taught hands-on

The program provides a foundation in the industrial instrumentation trade, with practical experience in applying, installing, repairing, calibrating and tuning measurement and control instruments. Delivery is in person, and half of the program is hands-on with industry-standard equipment.

Credential

Diploma, full-time

School

School of Energy, BCIT

Delivery

In person; half hands-on

Facilities & pathway

Practical work happens in the Spartan Controls Centre for Energy Education and Research — two common unit operations and a steam plant. Graduates work as instrumentation and control technicians and can enter an apprenticeship at an advanced level with an industry sponsor.

04HUST EE-E8 — overview

An advanced, English-taught program

EE-E8 is HUST's Advanced Program in Control Engineering and Automation, taught in English and accredited by ASIIN (international). It trains engineers to master modern control, robotics and automation technology, following international standards for a global career.

Program

EE-E8 · Advanced Program

Duration & credits

4 years · 132 credits

Accreditation

ASIIN (2023–2028)

11 programme learning outcomes (PLO)

The PLOs span mathematics and science; electrical, electronics, instrumentation and control foundations; design and optimisation of automatic systems; modelling and big-data solutions; modern software tools; data analysis and AI; English communication (TOEIC 600); critical thinking and lifelong learning; multidisciplinary teamwork; professional ethics and international standards; and research project management.
