BCIT Industrial Instrumentation & Process Control Technician

A full-time, in-person diploma from BCIT's School of Energy that builds a foundation in the industrial instrumentation trade: applying, installing, repairing, calibrating and tuning the measurement and control instruments used in industrial manufacturing.

01Program at a glance

A trade, taught hands-on

The program provides a foundation in the industrial instrumentation trade, with practical, hands-on experience in applying, installing, repairing, calibrating and tuning measurement and control instruments. Delivery is in person, and half of the program's duration is dedicated to hands-on work with industry-standard equipment.

Credential

Diploma, full-time

School

School of Energy, BCIT

Delivery

In person; half hands-on

What students do

Students install and maintain field instruments that are part of large-scale manufacturing systems — digital, electronic, pneumatic or hydraulic devices — and learn to program the computerized controls and programmable logic controllers (PLCs) that perform the required function within manufacturing.

02Curriculum coverage

The body of knowledge, term by term

Across its terms the diploma builds the instrumentation technician's core competencies — from electrical fundamentals and the four process variables through valves, PID control, PLCs and calibration. BCIT publishes the exact course matrix, course codes and credit values on the official program page.

Safety, codes & standards

Lockout/tagout, hazardous locations, electrical safety, and the codes that govern instrument installation in industrial plants.

Electrical & electronic fundamentals

Ohm's law, AC/DC circuits, semiconductors, op-amps and the signal electronics behind every transmitter and controller.

Measurement: temperature, pressure, flow, level

The four core process variables — thermocouples and RTDs, pressure and DP transmitters, orifice and Coriolis flow, radar and DP level.

Transducers & signal conditioning

Converting a physical quantity into 4–20 mA, HART, or a fieldbus value — and linearising, ranging and filtering it.

Control valves & actuators

Valve types, Cv sizing, positioners, and the final control element that turns a controller output into a physical change.

PID control & tuning

Proportional, integral and derivative action, loop stability, and the practical tuning methods used to make a loop behave.

PLCs & automation

Programming programmable logic controllers and computerized controls that run the required function within a manufacturing process.

Calibration, loop checking & documentation

Bench and field calibration, loop checkout, P&IDs, loop sheets and the records that prove a measurement is true.

03Facilities

The Spartan Controls Centre

Practical learning happens in the Spartan Controls Centre for Energy Education and Research — a world-class facility with two common unit operations and a steam plant, giving students realistic loops to instrument, calibrate and control.

Why it matters

A steam plant and real unit operations let students see the loops this site documents in action — drum level, combustion, flow and temperature control — rather than only on paper.

04Careers & pathway

Where graduates go

Graduates pursue employment as technicians in the instrumentation and control field across diverse industries, and can transition into an apprenticeship at an advanced level with an industry sponsor.
