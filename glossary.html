Glossary

Fifty-two terms that appear in both specifications and in meetings, with the meaning each discipline actually uses. Search works in either language.

4–20 mA4–20 mAThe analogue current loop standard. 4 mA is the live zero, 20 mA is full scale.
AccuracyĐộ chính xácHow close an indicated value is to the true value, under stated reference conditions.
ActuatorCơ cấu chấp hànhThe device that moves the final element — pneumatic diaphragm, electric motor, hydraulic ram.
AlarmCảnh báoAn audible or visible signal that requires an operator response, rationalised under ISA-18.2.
Anti-windupChống bão hòa tích phânLogic that stops the integral term accumulating while the output is saturated.
CalibrationHiệu chuẩnComparing an instrument against a traceable reference and adjusting zero and span.
Cascade controlĐiều khiển tầng nối tiếpA slow outer controller writes the setpoint of a fast inner controller.
CavitationXâm thựcVapour bubbles collapsing in a valve or orifice, causing noise, erosion and lost capacity.
Control valveVan điều khiểnA throttling valve with a positioner that accepts a control signal and modulates flow.
Cv / KvCv / KvFlow coefficients: Cv in US gpm at 1 psi drop, Kv in m³/h at 1 bar drop.
DCSDCS — Hệ điều khiển phân tánDistributed control system: plant-wide controllers, integrated engineering, history and alarms.
Dead bandDải chếtA change in input that produces no change in output. In valves this is stiction.
Dead timeThời gian trễThe interval between an input change and any observable response.
DriftĐộ trôiGradual change of zero or span over time, usually expressed per year.
ExtensometerExtensometer — đầu đo biến dạng dàiAn instrument measuring displacement along a line, typically in a borehole (ISO 18674-2).
FeedforwardĐiều khiển bù trướcActing on a measured disturbance before it produces an error.
Final control elementPhần tử chấp hành cuốiThe device that directly changes the process — valve, damper, drive, heater.
HARTHARTA digital signal superimposed on 4–20 mA for configuration and diagnostics.
HysteresisĐộ trễ (từ trễ)The difference between up-scale and down-scale readings at the same input.
IAEIAEIntegral of absolute error over time — a single number for loop performance.
InclinometerĐầu đo nghiêngAn instrument measuring lateral displacement with depth (ISO 18674-3).
Instrument airKhí nén điều khiểnClean, dry compressed air at 4–7 bar that drives pneumatic actuators.
Live zeroĐiểm không sốngA signal whose zero value is not 0 (4 mA), so a broken wire is distinguishable from a true zero.
LoopVòng điều khiểnSensor, controller and final element connected so the measurement affects the action.
MPCMPC — Điều khiển dự báo mô hìnhModel predictive control: optimises future moves over a horizon with constraints.
NAMUR NE 43NAMUR NE 43Defines 3.6–21.0 mA as the fault-signalling band outside the 4–20 mA range.
Observational methodPhương pháp quan trắcA design approach where monitoring confirms predictions and triggers pre-planned action (EN 1997-1 §2.7).
Orifice plateTấm lỗ khoanA bored plate that creates a pressure difference proportional to flow squared (ISO 5167).
P&IDP&ID — Sơ đồ đường ống và thiết bịPiping and instrumentation diagram: the drawing that defines tags, loops and connections.
PiezometerPiezometer — đầu đo áp lực nước lỗ rỗngAn instrument measuring pore water pressure u (ISO 18674-4).
PIDPID — Bộ điều khiển tỷ lệ–tích phân–vi phânThe three-term feedback algorithm: proportional, integral, derivative.
PLCPLC — Bộ điều khiển logic khả trìnhA rugged industrial computer executing cyclic logic, programmed per IEC 61131-3.
Pressure cellCảm biến áp tổngA flat cell measuring total stress in soil or against a structure (ISO 18674-5).
Process variableBiến quá trìnhThe quantity being controlled — the PV in the loop equation.
RangeabilityTỷ số điều chỉnhThe ratio of maximum to minimum usable measurement within the stated accuracy.
RedundancyDự phòngDuplicate or independent measurement paths so one failure does not lose the signal.
RepeatabilityĐộ lặp lạiThe spread of readings for the same input, approached from the same direction.
RTDRTD — Nhiệt kế điện trởA resistance temperature detector, usually platinum (Pt100), per IEC 60751.
SCADASCADASupervisory control and data acquisition: remote monitoring and command over wide areas.
SetpointGiá trị đặtThe target value the controller works to hold — the SP in the loop equation.
SettlementLúnDownward movement of a structure or ground surface, measured absolutely or relatively.
SILSIL — Mức toàn vẹn an toànSafety integrity level 1–4, a property of a complete safety function per IEC 61508/61511.
SISSIS — Hệ thống an toànSafety instrumented system: independent of the control system, brings the plant to a safe state.
StictionMa sát tĩnhStatic friction in a valve that must be overcome before any movement occurs.
Strain gaugeTenxơmetA foil or wire element whose resistance changes with strain (ISO 18674-7).
ThermocoupleCặp nhiệt điệnTwo dissimilar metals producing an emf at a junction, per IEC 60584.
ThermowellỐng bảo vệ nhiệtA closed pocket that isolates the sensor from the process while conducting heat to it.
TransmitterBộ truyền tín hiệuThe device that converts a sensor output into a standard signal.
Trigger-action planKế hoạch ngưỡng–hành độngA table of thresholds and the specific actions each one requires, agreed before construction.
TurndownTỷ số quay vòngHow far below full scale a device still meets its accuracy — often used interchangeably with rangeability.
Vibrating wireDây rungA sensing principle where a tensioned wire's frequency encodes pressure, strain or load.
Zero and spanZero và spanThe two calibration adjustments: the output at minimum input, and the output change per unit input.
