“Practical Process Control for Engineers and Technicians” opens by building the vocabulary of process dynamics — resistance, capacitance and inertia — and the time constants that govern every loop, before defining the signals (PV, SP, MV, OP) and the families of control (open, feedforward, feedback and cascade).
