Control Valve Primer — A User's Guide, 5th Edition

Hans D. Baumann · ISA / Wiley, 5th edition, 2011

Hans Baumann's primer is the ISA classic on control valves: a user-oriented, question-and-answer walk through valve types, sizing, cavitation, noise, actuators, positioners and digital fieldbus control. The fifth edition adds mathematical modelling of cavitation, smart positioners and loop-performance evaluation. The Vietnamese translation proceeds chapter by chapter; every figure is redrawn as a vector.
