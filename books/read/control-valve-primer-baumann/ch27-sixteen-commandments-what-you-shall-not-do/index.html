Sixteen Commandments: What You Shall Not Do! Sixteen Commandments: What You Shall Not Do!

You shall not:

  1. Install a steam valve at the bottom of a down-header.
  2. Install a steam valve at the bottom of a down-header without providing a steam trap near the inlet side of a valve.
  3. Locate a condensate return valve more than 3 ft away from a flash tank or condenser.
  4. Buy a cage-trim valve for elevated temperature service without elastic support between the cage and bonnet.
  5. Buy an I/P transducer with a 3 to 15 psi output signal when the actuator spring of the valve is also 3 to 15 psi and there is no positioner.
  6. Buy a flangeless, wafer-style control valve without a protective shroud where there is a fire hazard.
  7. Start up a control valve without first checking the packing tightness.
  8. Hydrostatically test a valve with bellows seal without first checking with the manufacturer.
  9. Install a single-seated globe valve with a spring-diaphragm actuator, flow-to-close, unless it has a small flow trim or it is used only for on-off service.
  10. Use carbon steel valve body material for saturated water (condensate) service.
  11. Install a valve in a pipe more than twice the diameter of the valve.
  12. Let a controller go unstable for too long; the result may be a leaky stem packing.
  13. Allow the valve outlet velocity to exceed 250 ft/s when you use a low-noise trim.
  14. Install a horizontally placed heavy actuator without proper support.
  15. Insulate a valve bonnet on a cryogenic valve.
  16. Plug the vent hole on a pneumatic actuator.
Source quirks. The chapter consists only of sixteen short numbered rules under the heading “You shall not,” with no accompanying explanation, figures, tables, equations or references. Original numbers and units (3 ft, 3–15 psi, 250 ft/s) are retained verbatim.
