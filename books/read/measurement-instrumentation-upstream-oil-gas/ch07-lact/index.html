What a LACT unit does

A lease automatic custody transfer (LACT) unit is the critical control point where produced oil is measured, verified, and transferred from the lease to a pipeline or purchaser, establishing ownership and revenue accountability. It integrates flow measurement, sampling, and quality verification into an automated system, ensuring consistent and traceable custody transfer. When measurement accuracy or system performance is compromised, it can directly impact revenue, reporting, and regulatory compliance, making reliable operation essential at this stage of production.

Ensuring Accurate Flow and Density Measurement for Custody Transfer

Measurement accuracy at the LACT is directly tied to revenue, with every barrel determining payment, making it the most critical fiscal point in upstream operations. Accuracy must be maintained across changing crude properties because any drift leads to financial errors. Emerson's Micro Motion ELITE Peak Performance Coriolis Flow and Density Meter (Figure 22), paired with a Micro Motion 5700 Field Mount Transmitter with net oil capability, delivers precise mass flow and density measurement to verify oil quality and detect water in the line.

Reliable Integrated LACT Systems for Fiscal Measurement Integrity

A LACT unit relies on multiple integrated measurement and control components, and using equipment from different vendors can introduce integration gaps, calibration issues, and unclear accountability at a critical fiscal point. A turnkey system from a single provider ensures flow measurement, compensation, proving, and controls are fully integrated and factory tested, reducing commissioning time and risk. Emerson's Liquid Metering Solutions (Figure 23) deliver customizable LACT skids that support accurate measurement, verify oil quality, and handle complex transactions — helping reduce fiscal risk, maintain compliance, and optimize performance.
