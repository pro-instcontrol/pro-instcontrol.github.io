Enabling Autonomous, Resilient Upstream Operations

Keeping well pads operating efficiently has always been a challenge for upstream companies.

The case for autonomy

Keeping well pads operating efficiently has always been a challenge for upstream companies. They're inhospitable with most equipment out of doors, they're in remote locations, and when problems occur, the effects are very costly. The desire to reduce or eliminate manual intervention is very strong as it is a major expense, hence the need for automation to keep things running smoothly and efficiently, while ensuring long-term reliability.

Instrumentation that survives the site

These sites also challenge automation for the same reasons. Any instrumentation deployed must stand up to harsh conditions and be able to operate for years, or even decades at a time, with little or no human interaction. Any pressure transmitters, temperature sensors, or flow meters must be highly durable with minimal maintenance required.

Emerson's role

Emerson understands these requirements. Our engineers are constantly working on developing products with that level of durability, but that are also intuitive and easy to work with.
