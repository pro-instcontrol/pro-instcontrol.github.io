Why injection gas measurement matters

Accurate per-well injection gas measurement is fundamental to optimizing lift efficiency and allocation. Without it, operators can't determine true gas-to-production ratios or identify underperforming wells. Injection gas streams are subject to pressure, temperature, and composition variability that affect measurement accuracy, so proper compensation and meter selection is essential to maintain reliable totalization across changing operating conditions.

Accurate Flow Measurement for Gas Lift Production Monitoring

Liquid carryover in injection gas can disrupt well performance, restrict flow, and accelerate valve wear, with water slugs often cleared by plunger lift systems controlled by the flow meter. All gas wells require real-time flow measurement for monitoring and control, with Emerson's Rosemount 4088 MultiVariable Transmitter (Figure 8) supporting plunger lift and production with extended range performance to capture pressure spikes while maintaining accuracy. For applications requiring mass flow measurement, Emerson's Micro Motion F Series Coriolis Meters with the 4700 Transmitter provides accurate flow and density data across a wide pressure range and can integrate with plunger lift systems, with optional Bluetooth connectivity improving safety and simplifying configuration.

FIGURE 8 Rosemount 4088 MultiVariable Transmitter (left) and Micro Motion F-Series Coriolis meter (right).

Reliable Non-Intrusive Flow Measurement for Sour Gas and High Pressure Service

Especially for high-pressure and large pipe diameters, Emerson's Flexim FLUXUS G731 and G831 clamp-on ultrasonic flow meters (Figure 9) provide reliable measurement without pressure limitations or process penetration. Mounted outside the pipe, they are unaffected by corrosive or toxic sour gas compositions such as H2S, while the integrated Dynamic Gas Meter feature automatically compensates for changing gas properties and compressibility to deliver reliable standard volume and mass flow data under varying operating conditions.

FIGURE 9 Flexim FLUXUS G731 and G831 use non-intrusive clamp-on ultrasonic technology.

Monitoring Injection Pressure to Maintain Gas Lift Efficiency

Precise injection pressure control is critical to lift efficiency, as over-injection wastes lift gas and under-injection reduces production, with monitoring at each injection point needed to detect uneven distribution or blockages. Emerson's Rosemount Wireless Pressure Gauge (Figure 10) provides reliable local pressure indication with a robust design that resists pressure spikes, up to 10 years of maintenance-free operation, and WirelessHART technology for remote data access without cabling.

FIGURE 10 Rosemount Wireless Pressure Gauge has no fragile bourdon tube or gears and sends data via WirelessHART.

Enabling Real-Time Control of Gas Injection Rates Without Costly Well Interventions

Beyond measurement, operators need the ability to actively optimize gas lift performance as reservoir conditions evolve. The Adaptive Gas Lift System (AGLS) (Figure 11) provides real-time control of gas injection rates through a retrievable electric gas lift valve, enabling continuous optimization without costly well interventions. Operators can respond quickly to production instabilities, optimize gas usage when injection gas is constrained, increase production from underperforming wells, enhancing well profitability throughout the lifetime of the well.

FIGURE 11 The low-power, electrically actuated gas lift valve enables continuous remote control of the choke opening.
