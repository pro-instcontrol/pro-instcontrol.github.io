Design Lifecycle

Paul Gruhn, P.E., CFSE & Harry L. Cheddie, P.Eng., CFSE

Chapter 2 · 11 pages
What this page is. An outline and figure companion to Chapter 2. The full text lives in 1829_02.pdf in the library catalogue and is not republished here. The complete Vietnamese translation of this chapter is here.

"If I had 8 hours to cut down a tree, I would spend 6 hours sharpening the axe." - A. Lincoln. Designing a safety instrumented system is beyond any single person: it demands a multi-discipline team. This chapter shows why - from hindsight vs. foresight and the HSE findings on accident causes (44% traced to wrong or incomplete specifications) through the eight steps of the SIS design lifecycle described in IEC 61511 / ANSI-ISA-84.
