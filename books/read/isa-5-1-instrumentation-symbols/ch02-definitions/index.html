Definitions

ANSI/ISA-5.1-1984 (R1992) · §3

Chapter 2 · 4 source pages · companion notes
Key terms. Bubble — the circular instrument/function symbol. · Loop — two or more instruments passing signals between them. · Sensor / primary element — the first element to sense a process variable. · Final control element — the device that manipulates the process. · Set point — the desired value of the controlled variable.

Clause 3 defines the standard's entire foundational vocabulary. These definitions apply for the purpose of understanding the standard, and terms italicised within a definition are also defined here. For a fuller treatment the standard refers to ISA-51.1 and the ISA-75 series.

Glossary §3 — Definitions

The table keeps the standard's English headwords — they are the source of the letter codes in Table 1 — and gives the Vietnamese equivalent together with the full definition.

AccessibleTruy cập đượcA device or function an operator can use or see in order to perform control actions, e.g. set-point changes, auto-manual transfer or on-off actions.
AlarmBáo độngA device or function that signals an abnormal condition by an audible or visible discrete change, or both, intended to attract attention. A device that merely opens or closes a circuit is properly called a switch (level switch, flow switch, …); it is an alarm only if it itself contains the alarm function.
AssignableCó thể gánA feature permitting a signal to be channelled from one device to another without switching, patching or wiring changes.

Behind the panelSau bảng điều khiểnA location within the area containing the instrument panel, its associated rack-mounted hardware, or enclosed within the panel. Such devices are not accessible for the operator's normal use; broadly equivalent to 'not normally accessible to the operator'.
BinaryNhị phânA signal or device having only two discrete positions or states. In its simplest sense (binary vs analog) it denotes an on-off or high-low state, i.e. one that does not represent a continuously varying quantity.
BoardBảngSynonym for panel.
BubbleVòng tròn ký hiệuThe circular symbol used to denote and identify the purpose of an instrument or function; it may contain a tag number. Synonym for balloon.
Computing deviceThiết bị tính toánA device or function that performs one or more calculations and/or logic operations and transmits one or more resulting output signals. Sometimes called a computing relay.
ConfigurableCó thể cấu hìnhA device or system whose functional characteristics can be selected or rearranged by programming or other methods; rewiring is excluded.
ControllerBộ điều khiểnA device whose output varies to regulate a controlled variable in a specified manner. It may be a self-contained analog or digital instrument or the equivalent in a shared-control system. An automatic controller responds to a measured process variable; a manual controller is a manual loading station. A controller may be integral with other loop elements.
Control stationTrạm điều khiểnA manual loading station that also switches between manual and automatic control modes of a loop; also called an auto-manual station. The operator interface of a distributed control system may be regarded as one.
Control valveVan điều khiểnA device, other than a common hand-actuated on-off valve or a self-actuated check valve, that directly manipulates the flow of one or more fluid process streams. 'Hand control valve' should be limited to hand-actuated valves used for throttling or requiring instrument identification.
ConverterBộ chuyển đổiA device that receives information in one form of instrument signal and transmits an output signal in another form. An instrument that changes a sensor output into a standard signal is a transmitter, not a converter (TE connects to TT, not TY). 'Transducer' is too general for this use.
DigitalSốA signal or device that uses binary digits to represent continuous values or discrete states.
Distributed control systemHệ thống điều khiển phân tánA system which, while functionally integrated, consists of subsystems that may be physically separate and remotely located from one another.
Final control elementPhần tử chấp hành cuốiThe device that directly controls the value of the manipulated variable of a control loop; often a control valve.
FunctionChức năngThe purpose of, or an action performed by, a device.
IdentificationNhận dạngThe sequence of letters or digits, or both, used to designate an individual instrument or loop.
InstrumentThiết bị (đo/điều khiển)A device used directly or indirectly to measure and/or control a variable. It includes primary elements, final control elements, computing devices and electrical devices such as annunciators, switches and pushbuttons. It does not apply to internal component parts (a receiver bellows or a resistor).
InstrumentationThiết bị đo lường (hệ thống)A collection of instruments, or their application, for observation, measurement, control or any combination of these.
LocalTại chỗAn instrument that is neither in nor on a panel or console, nor mounted in a control room; commonly near a primary element or a final control element. 'Field' is often used synonymously.
Local panelBảng tại chỗA panel that is not the central or main panel, commonly near plant subsystems or sub-areas. Not to be confused with 'local instrument'.
LoopVòng lặpTwo or more instruments or control functions arranged so that signals pass from one to another for measurement and/or control of a process variable.
Manual loading stationTrạm nạp tayA device or function with a manually adjustable output used to actuate one or more remote devices. It does not switch between manual and automatic modes; it may have integral indicators, lights or other features. Also called manual station or manual loader.
MeasurementPhép đoThe determination of the existence or the magnitude of a variable.
MonitorThiết bị giám sátA general, rather unspecific term for an instrument or instrument system used to measure or sense the status or magnitude of variables in order to derive useful information; it may mean analyser, indicator or alarm. Also used as a verb.
Monitor lightĐèn giám sátSynonym for pilot light.
PanelBảng điều khiểnA structure carrying a group of instruments, housing the operator–process interface and chosen to have a unique designation; it may consist of sections, cubicles, consoles or desks. Synonym for board.
Panel-mountedLắp trên bảngAn instrument mounted on a panel or console and accessible for the operator's normal use. A function normally accessible to the operator in a shared-display system is the equivalent.
Pilot lightĐèn báo trạng tháiA light indicating which of a number of normal conditions of a system or device exists; unlike an alarm light, which indicates an abnormal condition. Also called monitor light.
Primary elementPhần tử sơ cấpSynonym for sensor.
ProcessQuá trìnhAny operation or sequence of operations involving a change of energy, state, composition, dimension or other properties definable with respect to a datum.
Process variableBiến quá trìnhAny variable property of a process. In this standard the term applies to all variables other than instrument signals.
ProgramChương trìnhA repeatable sequence of actions that defines the status of outputs as a fixed relationship to a set of inputs.
Programmable logic controllerBộ điều khiển logic khả trình (PLC)A controller, usually with multiple inputs and outputs, that contains an alterable program.
RelayRelay / rơ-leA device that passes information on unchanged or in modified form; often used to mean computing device, though the latter is preferred. Also specifically an electric, pneumatic or hydraulic switch actuated by a signal.
ScanQuétTo sample, in a predetermined manner, each of a number of variables intermittently; often to ascertain a variable's state or value, and possibly associated with recording or alarming.
SensorCảm biếnThe part of a loop or instrument that first senses the value of a process variable and assumes a corresponding, predetermined and intelligible state or output. It may be separate from or integral with another loop element. Also called detector or primary element.
Set pointGiá trị đặtAn input variable that sets the desired value of the controlled variable; it may be set manually, automatically or by program, and is expressed in the same units as the controlled variable.
Shared controllerBộ điều khiển dùng chungA controller containing preprogrammed algorithms that are usually accessible, configurable and assignable; it permits a number of process variables to be controlled by a single device.
Shared displayHiển thị dùng chungThe operator interface device (usually a video screen) used to display process control information from a number of sources at the operator's command.
SwitchCông tắcA device that connects, disconnects, selects or transfers one or more circuits and is not designated as a controller, relay or control valve. Also used as a verb for the functions performed.
Test pointĐiểm thửA process connection to which no instrument is permanently connected but which is intended for temporary or intermittent connection of an instrument.
TransducerBộ biến đổi (transducer)A general term for a device that receives information as one or more physical quantities, modifies the information and/or its form if required, and produces a resultant output signal. Depending on application it may be a primary element, transmitter, relay, converter or other device; being unspecific, its use for specific applications is not recommended.
TransmitterBộ truyền tín hiệuA device that senses a process variable through a sensor and has an output whose steady-state value varies only as a predetermined function of that variable; the sensor may or may not be integral with it.

Two distinctions worth keeping Commonly confused pairs

  • Alarm ↔ switch. A device that merely opens or closes a circuit is a level switch, flow switch and so on; it is an alarm only if it contains the alarm function.
  • Transmitter ↔ converter. Converting a sensor output to a standard signal is a transmitter (TT); a converter (TY) changes one form of instrument signal into another.
  • Pilot light ↔ alarm light. A pilot light indicates which normal condition exists; an alarm light indicates an abnormal condition.
  • Local ↔ behind the panel. «Local» means outside the panel/control room, usually near a sensor or valve; «behind the panel» means within the panel area and not accessible for normal operator use.
Where this leads. These definitions are the key to Table 1 (identification letters) in Chapter 4 and to the symbol sheets in Chapter 6: E is «Sensor (Primary Element)», T is «Transmit», V is «Valve, Damper, Louver», Z is «Driver, Actuator, Unclassified Final Control Element».
