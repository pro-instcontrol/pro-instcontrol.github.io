About the appendices

The book closes with five reference appendices: abbreviations of organizations and standards related to the international valve industry (A), properties of fluids (B), standards pertaining to valves (C), the International System of Units (D), and a valve glossary (E). This unit summarizes the appendices at quick-reference depth; the full data tables are in the source book.

Appendix B — Properties of fluids

Appendix B contains three compressibility factor charts of Z against reduced pressure Pr (B-1 for Pr = 0 to 0.5, B-2 for Pr = 0 to 10, B-3 for Pr = 0 to 40) and Table B-1 with the physical constants of gases and vapors: molecular mass, individual gas constant, isentropic exponent k, critical temperature Tc and critical pressure Pc. These charts serve the sizing of pressure relief devices in Unit 17: the compressibility factor is applied when the operating pressure approaches the critical region, where real-gas behavior departs from the ideal-gas model.

Appendix D — International System of Units (SI)

The SI system is built on seven base units and two supplementary units; derived units are expressed in terms of these or of other derived units, and decimal multiples are formed with prefixes. When working with US valve documentation, the conversion factors in Table D-7 of the source book convert between Imperial, metric, and SI units for length, area, volume, volume flow, mass, mass flow, pressure, temperature, energy, and power.
