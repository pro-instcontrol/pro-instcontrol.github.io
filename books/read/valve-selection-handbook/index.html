Valve Selection Handbook — Engineering Fundamentals for Selecting Manual Valves, Check Valves, Pressure Relief Valves and Rupture Discs

Peter Smith & R. W. Zappe · 5th edition, Gulf Professional Publishing / Elsevier, 2004

21 chapters · 387 pages · 1 PDF files
🟢 Fully translated · 21 of 21 chapters

100% complete

The standard industry reference on valve selection: how the sealing mechanism, flow behaviour (pressure loss, cavitation, waterhammer, noise) and closure-member kinematics of each valve family drive the choice for a given process service. Covers manual valves (globe, gate, plug, ball, butterfly, pinch, diaphragm), check valves, pressure relief valves, rupture discs, sizing, actuators, double block & bleed, and mechanical locking. The Vietnamese translation proceeds unit by unit; every cutaway and chart is redrawn as a vector.
