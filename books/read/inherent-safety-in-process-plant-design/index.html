Inherent Safety in Process Plant Design — An Index-Based Approach

Anna-Mari Heikkilä · VTT Publications 384, Technical Research Centre of Finland, 1999

Anna-Mari Heikkilä's VTT thesis on inherent safety: rather than controlling hazards with add-on protective systems, an inherently safer plant avoids them by using less hazardous materials and operations. The work builds the Inherent Safety Index for conceptual chemical process design, splits it into chemical and process subindices, evaluates process structure by case-based reasoning, and uses the index as a fitness function in genetic optimisation. The Vietnamese translation proceeds chapter by chapter; every figure, flowsheet and scoring table is redrawn as a vector.
