Displacement Measurement, Linear and Angular

Keith Antonelli et al. · 12 sections

Chapter 6 · 167 source pages · 12 sections · 63,000 words
Why this chapter is twelve pages. Chapter 6 runs to 167 pages and about 63,000 words — ten times a normal chapter. To keep it readable on the web, each of sections 6.1–6.12 is translated and published as its own page, with previous / next navigation between them. This page is the chapter index.

Displacement is the root quantity behind most industrial measurement: valve position, piston stroke, thickness, vibration, level and shaft angle all reduce to it. The chapter walks through twelve sensor families, from the cheap potentiometer to the laser interferometer that counts half wavelengths, and for each one states the principle, the limits and how to choose.

The twelve sections

  • 6.1Resistive Displacement SensorsThe potentiometer: voltage divider, linearity, loading error, wear and mounting.
  • 6.2Inductive Displacement SensorsLVDT, RVDT, variable-reluctance and eddy-current sensors, and EMI shielding.
  • 6.3Capacitive Sensors — DisplacementDifferential capacitance, smart sensors, pressure, accelerometers, level.
  • 6.4Piezoelectric Transducers and SensorsPiezoelectricity: governing equations, materials, measurement, applications.
  • 6.5Laser Interferometer Displacement SensorsMichelson interferometry with a He–Ne laser and the refractive index of air.
  • 6.6Bore Gaging Displacement SensorsBore diameter measurement: principles, heads, errors and shop-floor practice.
  • 6.7Time-of-Flight Ultrasonic Displacement SensorsTime-of-flight ultrasound: pulses, transducers, noise and resolution limits.
  • 6.8Optical Encoder Displacement SensorsAbsolute and incremental optical encoders, quadrature, interpolation, decoding.
  • 6.9Magnetic Displacement SensorsMagnetostrictive, magnetoresistive, Hall and magnetic encoder sensors.
  • 6.10Synchro/Resolver Displacement SensorsSynchros and resolvers: rotary transformers, R/D conversion, type II servo loops.
  • 6.11Optical Fiber Displacement SensorsFiber Bragg gratings, extrinsic and intrinsic Fabry–Perot, four schemes compared.
  • 6.12Optical Beam Deflection SensingPosition-sensitive detectors and the measurement of optical beam deflection.
