Calorimetry Measurement

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 36 · Calorimetry Measurement · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 36. The source prose is not repeated; the governing relations and reference data are summarised below.

Calorimetry reduces to an energy balance Q = C_eff*DeltaT, with the effective heat capacity of vessel plus contents established by electrical or chemical calibration (benzoic acid, ≈ 26.43 MJ·kg⁻¹). Adiabatic, isoperibol, isothermal and heat-flow designs trade mechanical complexity against correction complexity. Combustion bombs give gross/net heat of combustion (ASTM D240/D5865); DSC gives T_m, T_g and transition enthalpy; ITC yields DeltaH, binding constant K_a and stoichiometry. Indium (156.6 °C, 28.71 J·g⁻¹) is the standard temperature/enthalpy reference.

(36.1)Q = C_eff·ΔT , dQ/dt = K·(T_cell − T_jacket) , C_eff = (V·I·t)/ΔT
