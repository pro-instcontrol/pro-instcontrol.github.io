Laser Output Measurement

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 63 · Laser Output Measurement · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 63. Equations and table of the source are reproduced below; the prose is not repeated.

Laser output measurement covers power (W) or pulse energy (J), wavelength, beam quality M², beam diameter/profile, stability and spectrum. Power: a thermopile absorbs everything (broad range, CW and long pulses), a photodiode is fast and sensitive (small powers, spectral), and a calorimeter is the primary standard. Pulse energy uses a pyroelectric detector or joulemeter; high-repetition-rate pulses use fast photodiodes. Beam quality M² = π·w₀·θ/λ (w₀ waist, θ divergence; M² = 1 ideal Gaussian) is measured by focusing through a lens and profiling the beam (CCD/camera or scanning-knife-edge) at several axial positions z; beam profiles reveal hotspots and guide optics alignment. Wavelength: a wavemeter (Fizeau/Michelson interferometer) reaches 10⁻⁶; the spectrum (line width) is measured by a Fabry–Perot or an optical spectrum analyzer (OSA) — required for WDM and spectroscopic lasers.
