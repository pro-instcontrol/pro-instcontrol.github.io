Colorimetry

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 58 · Colorimetry · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 58. Equations and table of the source are reproduced below; the prose is not repeated.

Color is specified by CIE 1931 tristimulus values X, Y, Z — integrals of source × object × standard matching functions x̄, ȳ, z̄ — with chromaticity coordinates x = X/(X+Y+Z), y = Y/(X+Y+Z). The CIE L*a*b* space (L* lightness, a* green–red, b* blue–yellow) is more perceptual; color difference is ΔE*ab = √((ΔL*)²+(Δa*)²+(Δb*)²): <1 imperceptible, 1–3 perceptible but acceptable, >3 obvious. A colorimeter uses three x̄,ȳ,z̄ filters for direct XYZ — fast and cheap for print, paint, food QC. A spectrophotometer measures the full reflectance/transmittance spectrum (400–700 nm, 5–10 nm steps) and integrates to tristimulus — more accurate, any color space, also used for emissive displays and lamps. Applications: print ΔE vs standard, paint/plastics, food color, display gamut/ΔE, water, and quantitative colorimetric chemistry.
