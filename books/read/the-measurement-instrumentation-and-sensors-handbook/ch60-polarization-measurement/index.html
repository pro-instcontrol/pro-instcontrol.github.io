Polarization Measurement

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 60 · Polarization Measurement · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 60. Equations and table of the source are reproduced below; the prose is not repeated.

Light can be linearly, circularly or elliptically polarized; the state is described by the Jones vector or the four Stokes parameters (S₀–S₃), all measurable with polarizer + retarder combinations. A rotating polarizer before the detector yields a sine/cosine signal whose amplitude and phase give S₁, S₂; adding a rotating quarter-wave retarder gives S₃, so the full state, degree of polarization P = √(S₁²+S₂²+S₃²)/S₀ and axis follow. Retardance of thin films/LCD cells is δ = 2π·Δn·d/λ, measured spectrally between crossed polarizers. In fiber, polarization-mode dispersion (PMD) splits the two orthogonal modes — measured by interferometry or swept-wavelength Stokes analysis (ps/√km) — and polarization-dependent loss (PDL) of components is measured across states. Applications: stress birefringence, displays, chirality, telecom.
