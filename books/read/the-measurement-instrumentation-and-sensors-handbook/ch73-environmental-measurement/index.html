Environmental Measurement

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 73 · Environmental Measurement · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 73. The table of the source is reproduced below; the prose is not repeated.

Environmental monitoring covers air, water, soil and noise for health protection and compliance. Criteria air pollutants: SO₂ (UV fluorescence — absorption at 214 nm and re-emitted fluorescence ∝ concentration), NO/NOx (chemiluminescence — NO+O₃→NO₂* emits light; NOx after an NO₂→NO converter), O₃ (UV photometric at 254 nm), CO (NDIR) — ppb-level analyzers meeting US EPA/EN reference methods. Particulate matter PM10/PM2.5: beta attenuation (C-14 through a filter tape), TEOM (tapered-element oscillating microbalance), optical scattering (low-cost sensors), and gravimetric reference. Water: pH, dissolved oxygen (membrane or luminescence), conductivity, COD/BOD, NO₃⁻/NH₄⁺, heavy metals (ICP-MS), turbidity (Ch. 62). Soil: pH, heavy metals, moisture, organics. Noise: dB(A) sound level meters per IEC 61672 for 24-h urban monitoring; stations aggregate into AQI.
