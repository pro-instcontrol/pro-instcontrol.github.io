Spectrum Analysis and Correlation

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 83. The source prose is not repeated; the governing relations and reference data are summarised below.

Spectral analysis uses the DFT/FFT with bin spacing Δf = f_s/N = 1/T — resolution depends on record length, not zero padding. Windowing (Hann, Hamming, Blackman–Harris) suppresses leakage sidelobes at the cost of main-lobe width and requires power correction. Welch's averaged periodogram trades resolution for variance. The Wiener–Khinchin theorem links autocorrelation to PSD; cross-correlation yields time delay and H(f) = S_xy/S_xx; magnitude-squared coherence γ² validates FRF measurements.
