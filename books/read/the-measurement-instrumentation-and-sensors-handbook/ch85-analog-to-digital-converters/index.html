Analog-to-Digital Converters

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 85 · Analog-to-Digital Converters · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 85. The source prose is not repeated; the governing relations and reference data are summarised below.

An ADC samples, quantises and codes: f_s > 2 f_max with an anti-alias filter, q = V_FS/2^N, and ideal SNR = 6.02N + 1.76 dB for a full-scale sine. Real performance is captured by SINAD and ENOB = (SINAD − 1.76)/6.02, degraded by thermal noise, clock jitter (SNR = −20 log(2π f_in σ_t)), DNL/INL and reference drift. Flash/pipeline suit multi-GS/s capture, SAR suits multiplexed medium-speed measurements, sigma–delta oversampling with noise shaping reaches 16–24 bit, and dual-slope integrating converters reject 50/60 Hz line noise in DMMs. Two-point offset/gain calibration and a quiet, decoupled V_ref are mandatory for accurate work.

(85.1)f_s > 2·f_max , f_alias = |f_in − k·f_s| , q = V_FS/2^N (LSB)
(85.2)SNR_ideal = 6,02·N + 1,76 dB , SINAD = 20·log₁₀(S/(N+D)) , ENOB = (SINAD − 1,76)/6,02
