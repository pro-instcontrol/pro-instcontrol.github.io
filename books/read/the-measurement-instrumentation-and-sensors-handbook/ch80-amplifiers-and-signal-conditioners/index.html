Amplifiers and Signal Conditioners

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 80 · Amplifiers and Signal Conditioners · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 80. The source prose is not repeated; the governing relations and reference data are summarised below.

Signal conditioning amplifies, isolates, filters and scales µV–mV sensor outputs to the ADC input range. The three-op-amp instrumentation amplifier gives G = 1 + 2R/R_G with very high input impedance and CMRR > 100 dB; resistor matching largely sets the actual CMRR. Isolation amplifiers break ground loops (2–5 kV), chopper/auto-zero stages remove offset drift, ratiometric bridge excitation cancels supply drift, and 3-/4-wire connections compensate lead resistance. Johnson noise sqrt(4kTRB), 1/f noise and 50/60 Hz pickup set the floor; anti-alias filtering and 4–20 mA current loops complete the chain.

(80.1)G = 1 + 2R/R_G , CMRR_dB = 20·log₁₀(A_d/A_cm) , V_o = G·(V₂ − V₁) + V_ref
(80.2)V_bridge ≈ V_ex·(ΔR/(4R)) , V_o ∝ V_ex·ε·G (cảm biến biến dạng)
(80.3)v_n,rms = √(4kTRB) , NF = 10·log₁₀(SNR_in/SNR_out) , f_c = 1/(2πRC)
