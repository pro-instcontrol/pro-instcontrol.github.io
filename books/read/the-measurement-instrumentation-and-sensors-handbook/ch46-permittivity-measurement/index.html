Permittivity Measurement

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 46 · Permittivity Measurement · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 46. Equations and table of the source are reproduced below; the prose is not repeated.

Permittivity ε = ε₀ε_r (ε₀ ≈ 8.854×10⁻¹² F/m); ε_r is the relative permittivity. Measurement is usually paired with the loss tangent tanδ = G/(ωC), a sensitivity indicator for ageing, moisture and contamination in dielectrics. A parallel-plate cell gives ε_r = C·d/(ε₀·A), measured by bridge or LCR meter at low-to-mid frequency (corrections for fringing and thickness needed). The Schering bridge yields C_x and G_x simultaneously, hence ε_r and tanδ — central to insulation testing (transformer oil, cables). At RF/microwave, a re-entrant or resonant cavity holding the sample shifts the resonance frequency f and quality factor Q, from which ε_r and tanδ are derived; free-space methods transmit a wave through the sample and infer ε_r from attenuation and phase shift.
