Thermal Imaging

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 35 · Thermal Imaging · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 35. The source prose is not repeated; the governing relations and reference data are summarised below.

Thermal cameras convert band-limited infrared radiance into an apparent temperature. Planck's law, Stefan–Boltzmann T^4 and Wien's displacement law set the physics; the 3–5 µm and 8–14 µm atmospheric windows set the optics. The measured radiance mixes emitted, reflected and atmospheric terms, so emissivity and reflected apparent temperature dominate accuracy. Uncooled VOx/a-Si microbolometers dominate 8–14 µm systems; photon detectors (InSb, MCT) are faster but cryogenic. NETD, IFOV, MRTD and two-point blackbody calibration with NUC define performance.
