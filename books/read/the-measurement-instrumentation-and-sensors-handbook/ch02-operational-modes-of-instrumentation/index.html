Operational Modes of Instrumentation

Richard S. Figliola · Clemson University

Chapter 2
What this page is. An outline and figure companion to Chapter 2. The full text lives in Ch02.pdf in the library catalogue and is not republished here. The complete Vietnamese translation of this chapter is here.

A measuring instrument can operate in several modes. This chapter contrasts the null method (balancing the measurand against a known standard to reach a zero condition, with very high input impedance and low loading error) with the deflection method (output deflects proportionally to the measurand, drawing energy and so introducing loading error), then treats analog vs. digital sensors and readouts, and shows why input impedance governs loading error in a signal chain.
