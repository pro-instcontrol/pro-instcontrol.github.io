Photometry and Radiometry

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 56 · Photometry and Radiometry · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 56. The tables of the source are reproduced below; the prose is not repeated.

Radiometry measures optical power absolutely (W); photometry weights by the eye response V(λ) (lm = 683·V(λ)·W). Corresponding quantities: radiant flux Φ ↔ luminous flux Φ_v (lm), radiant intensity I ↔ luminous intensity I_v (cd), irradiance E ↔ illuminance E_v (lux), radiance L ↔ luminance L_v (cd/m²). Detectors: Si photodiodes (200–1100 nm, linear standards), Ge/InGaAs (900–1700 nm), thermopiles (broadband); photometric units add a V(λ) filter or corrected Si response. Handheld meters read lux, cd/m² or W/m²; goniophotometers sweep the angular distribution (IES/LDT); integrating spheres collect the total flux of a source. Calibration against NMI-traceable lamps or detectors, 1%–5% typical uncertainty.
