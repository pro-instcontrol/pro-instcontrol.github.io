Dosimetry Measurement

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 69. Equations and table of the source are reproduced below; the prose is not repeated.

Absorbed dose D = dE/dm in gray (Gy = J/kg); equivalent dose H = D·w_R in sievert (Sv) with radiation weighting w_R (γ, β: 1; protons: 2; α: 20); effective dose E = Σ w_T·H_T weights organs. Ion chambers measure dose rate linearly (µSv/h, mGy/min) — the standards-laboratory instrument; survey meters use ion chambers or GM tubes energy-compensated for H*(10); phantoms calibrate radiotherapy. Passive dosimeters: TLD (LiF — traps release light when heated, ∝ dose), OSL (Al₂O₃:C, laser-read, re-readable, the modern personnel-badge standard), film badges (legacy record), and CR-39 nuclear-track for α/neutron. Active dosimeters use Si diodes/MOSFETs or small ion chambers for real-time readout with alarm thresholds (entry-control, in-vivo radiotherapy dosimetry).
