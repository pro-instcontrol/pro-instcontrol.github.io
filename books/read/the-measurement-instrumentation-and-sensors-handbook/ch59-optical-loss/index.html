Optical Loss

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 59 · Optical Loss · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 59. Equations and table of the source are reproduced below; the prose is not repeated.

Optical loss Loss (dB) = −10·log₁₀(P_out/P_in); fiber attenuation α (dB/km) comes from absorption (OH⁻, impurities), Rayleigh scattering, nonlinearity and bend loss (silica floor ≈0.2 dB/km at 1550 nm). The cut-back method measures P_out over length L, trims the fiber and measures again to get α = (10/(L−L₂))·log₁₀(P₂/P_out) — the laboratory reference. An OTDR injects a pulse and measures backscatter vs time, locating features at z = c·t/(2n): attenuation profile, splices, connectors and breaks — the field-maintenance tool. Connector loss (0.1–0.5 dB) arises from offset, gap and end-face angle; return loss RL = −10log₁₀(P_ref/P_in) (50–60 dB is good); insertion loss characterizes inserted elements such as filters and WDM devices.
