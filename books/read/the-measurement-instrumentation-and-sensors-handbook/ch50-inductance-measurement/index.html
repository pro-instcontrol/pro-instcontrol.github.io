Inductance Measurement

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 50 · Inductance Measurement · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 50. Equations and table of the source are reproduced below; the prose is not repeated.

Inductance L = Φ/I = NΦ/I (henry). Every real coil also has series resistance R_s (loss) and stray capacitance, so measurement must separate the reactive part. Bridges balance the coil (L_x in series with R_x) against a standard capacitor: the Maxwell bridge uses a parallel RC arm giving a frequency-independent balance at line frequency with L_x = R₁R₃C_s; the Hay bridge uses an RL standard arm; the Anderson bridge adds resistors to cancel stray-capacitance error and is the most accurate. Resonance methods use f₀ = 1/(2π√LC) to back out L from a known C — good at high frequency. LCR meters automate L, R, Q, D over 20 Hz–2 MHz via auto-balancing or complex I–V. The Q-meter sweeps a series-LC resonator at fixed V_in and reads V_C = Q·V_in across the standard capacitor, giving Q = V_C/V_in and L = 1/((2πf)²C); Q = ωL/R_s.
