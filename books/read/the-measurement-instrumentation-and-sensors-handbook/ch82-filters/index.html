Filters

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 82 · Filters · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 82. The source prose is not repeated; the governing relations and reference data are summarised below.

Filters shape amplitude and phase in a measurement chain. A first-order RC has H(s) = 1/(1+sRC) and f_c = 1/(2πRC); order n gives 20n dB/decade. Butterworth is maximally flat, Chebyshev and elliptic trade ripple for steepness, Bessel preserves group delay and pulse shape. Notch filters remove 50/60 Hz; switched-capacitor filters set f_c from a clock; FIR offers exact linear phase and unconditional stability while IIR is cheaper but coefficient-sensitive. An anti-aliasing filter must precede the ADC with f_c < f_s/2, and the filter's equivalent noise bandwidth sets the output noise.
