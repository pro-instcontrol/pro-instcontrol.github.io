Permeability and Hysteresis Measurement

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 49 · Permeability and Hysteresis Measurement · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 49. Equations and table of the source are reproduced below; the prose is not repeated.

Permeability μ_r = μ/μ₀ = B/(μ₀H). The B–H hysteresis loop reveals the remanence B_r, coercivity H_c, initial permeability μ_i and maximum permeability μ_max; the loop area is the energy dissipated per cycle (core loss). A closed toroidal sample avoids flux leakage: N₁ primary turns carry I giving H = N₁I/l (l = circumference), and an N₂ secondary senses e = −N₂A·dB/dt; integrating e yields B(t), so two integrators trace the B–H loop on a scope or digitizer. Magnetic bridges (Maxwell, Hay, Anderson) model the cored coil as L_x in series with loss R_x and balance to give L_x, R_x, hence μ_r and the loss factor. Core loss = loop area × frequency.
