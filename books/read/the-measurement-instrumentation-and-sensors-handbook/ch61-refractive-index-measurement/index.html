Refractive Index Measurement

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 61 · Refractive Index Measurement · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 61. Equations and table of the source are reproduced below; the prose is not repeated.

Refractive index n = c/v governs refraction, total internal reflection and optical bandwidth; it varies with wavelength (dispersion) and temperature, hence values are quoted at n_D (589 nm). The critical-angle (Abbe) refractometer places a sample drop on a higher-index prism and reads θ_c = arcsin(n_sample/n_prism) through an eyepiece or sensor — four decimal places; handheld versions read Brix for sugar. The minimum-deviation prism method gives n = sin((A+δ_min)/2)/sin(A/2) with the highest accuracy (10⁻⁵–10⁻⁶) for calibration and optical materials. Ellipsometry measures polarization changes (Ψ, Δ) at oblique incidence to derive n, absorption k and film thickness (Å-level) — the thin-film standard for semiconductors and optical coatings.
