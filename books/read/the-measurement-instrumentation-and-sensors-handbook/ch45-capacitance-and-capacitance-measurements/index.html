Capacitance and Capacitance Measurements

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 45 · Capacitance and Capacitance Measurements · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 45. Equations and table of the source are reproduced below; the prose is not repeated.

Capacitance C = Q/V (farad); for a parallel plate C = ε₀ε_r A/d. Capacitance (pF–µF) is measured to derive permittivity, humidity, position and pressure (capacitive sensors) and to test components. A Wheatstone bridge compares C_x with a standard C_ref; the Schering bridge is specialised for capacitance and dielectric loss — it yields C_x and the loss tangent tanδ directly. Practical meters embed C in an oscillator (f = 1/2π√LC) or an RC timer, or use charge-balancing: charging C at a fixed frequency f gives I_avg = C·V·f, so C = I_avg/(V·f). Stray capacitance and leakage resistance are suppressed by guarding — a driven guard ring at neutral potential shunts leakage current away. LCR meters automate C/R/L measurement from mΩ–GΩ and pF–F over 20 Hz–2 MHz.
