Ventilation Measurement

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 77 · Ventilation Measurement · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 77. The source prose is not repeated; the governing relations and reference data are summarised below.

Ventilation measurement combines volume/flow (volume-displacement spirometers, Fleisch/Lilly pneumotachographs with Q = DeltaP/R and V = integral Q dt) with gas analysis (infrared capnography at 4.26 µm, paramagnetic/zirconia O₂). Minute ventilation is f*V_T ≈ 6 L/min; FEV₁/FVC > 0.70 distinguishes obstructive from restrictive disease. Residual volume and FRC cannot be measured by spirometry and require helium dilution, nitrogen washout or body plethysmography (Boyle's law). ATS/ERS requires 3 L syringe calibration within ± 3 % and BTPS correction.

(77.2)P₁V₁ = P₂V₂ ⇒ FRC = (ΔV/ΔP_mouth)·(P_bar − P_H₂O)
