Heat Flux

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 34. The source prose is not repeated; the governing relations and reference data are summarised below.

A heat-flux gauge is a calibrated thermal resistance layer with an embedded thermopile: q'' = k*DeltaT/delta and U = N*S*DeltaT. Schmidt–Boelter gauges are the fire-test reference; Gardon circular-foil gauges respond in about 0.1 s but depend on surface absorptivity; thin-film gauges suit curved hot surfaces; large HFS plates serve building envelopes. Calibration follows ISO 14934 / ASTM C1130. Mounting perturbations (emissivity mismatch, adhesive layer, lead conduction) usually dominate the error, so temperature compensation and matched coating are essential.
