Modulation

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 81 · Modulation · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 81. The source prose is not repeated; the governing relations and reference data are summarised below.

Modulation translates a measurement signal to a carrier for transmission, multiplexing and 1/f-noise avoidance. AM (m ≤ 1, B = 2 f_m) with synchronous demodulation is the basis of lock-in detection, where the low-pass time constant sets the equivalent noise bandwidth. FM/PM trade bandwidth (Carson: B ≈ 2(Δf + f_m), beta = Δf/f_m) for amplitude noise immunity. Pulse schemes (PAM, PWM, PPM, PCM) lead to sampling and sigma–delta oversampling with noise shaping; Nyquist f_s ≥ 2 f_max must always be respected with an anti-alias filter.
