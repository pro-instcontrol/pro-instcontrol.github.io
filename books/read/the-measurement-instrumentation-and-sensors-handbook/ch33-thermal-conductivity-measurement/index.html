Thermal Conductivity Measurement

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 33 · Thermal Conductivity Measurement · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 33. The source prose is not repeated; the governing relations and reference data are summarised below.

Thermal conductivity k (W·m⁻¹·K⁻¹) is obtained either from steady-state Fourier conduction (ASTM C177 guarded hot plate, ASTM C518 heat-flow meter) or from transient diffusivity measurements (hot wire, ASTM E1461 laser flash) with k = alpha*rho*cp. Guarded hot plate reaches 1–2 % for insulators but needs hours to equilibrate; laser flash is fast and suits metals. Contact resistance, edge convection, radiation through porous samples, moisture and anisotropy dominate the uncertainty budget.

(33.1)q = −k ∇T ⇒ k = (P·L)/(2·A·ΔT) (hai mẫu đối xứng, mỗi mẫu nhận P/2)
(33.2)ΔT = (Q/(4πk))·ln(t₂/t₁) , α = 0,1388·L²/t₁/₂ , k = α·ρ·c_p
