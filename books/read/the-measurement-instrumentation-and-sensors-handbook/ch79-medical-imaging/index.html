Medical Imaging

John G. Webster (chủ biên) · CRC Press, 1999

Chapter 79 · Medical Imaging · 0 figures, 1 tables

Companion to the Vietnamese translation of Chapter 79. The source prose is not repeated; the governing relations and reference data are summarised below.

Medical imaging is quantitative measurement: radiography/CT follow exponential attenuation and the Hounsfield scale HU = 1000(µ − µ_water)/µ_water; MRI encodes position via gradients at the Larmor frequency (42.58 MHz/T) and reconstructs by Fourier inversion of k-space; ultrasound ranges echoes with z = c*t/2, c ≈ 1540 m/s; PET detects coincident 511 keV annihilation photons. Image quality is specified by SNR, CNR, MTF, NPS and DQE, with the CT noise ∝ 1/sqrt(dose) trade-off and ALARA dose optimisation, verified by Catphan/ACR phantoms.
