Environmental protection describes the ability of an enclosure to keep dirt, dust, water, ice and corrosive agents out. Printed A7 compares specific enclosure applications; printed A8 explains the two-digit IP code.

Ingress Protection (IP) Ingress Protection (IP)

The IEC uses the term "Ingress Protection" to identify the environmental protection of a device. This is defined in IEC Standard 60 529 and the chart below illustrates the two-digit code used.

The IP classification system designates, by means of a number, the degree of protection provided by a device against ingress of dust and water. The first number (0-6) is the degree of protection against solid objects; the second number (0-8) is the degree of protection against water.
