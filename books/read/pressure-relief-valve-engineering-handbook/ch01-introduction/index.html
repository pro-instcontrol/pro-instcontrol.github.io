Introduction to the Crosby Engineering Handbook

Crosby Valve Inc. · Technical Document TP-V300

Chapter 1 · 4 source pages · companion notes

The Crosby Pressure Relief Valve Engineering Handbook contains important technical information relating to pressure relief valves.

The primary purpose of a pressure relief valve is protection of life and property by venting fluid from an overpressurized vessel. The information in this handbook applies to the overpressure protection of pressure vessels, lines and systems.

Reference is made to the ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code, Section VIII, Pressure Vessels. The information in this handbook is not to be used for the application of overpressure protection to power boilers and nuclear power plant components, which are addressed in the ASME Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code, Section I (Power Boilers) and Section III (Nuclear Power Plant Components), respectively.

Proper sizing, selection, manufacture, assembly, test, installation and maintenance of a pressure relief valve are all critical to obtaining maximum protection.

Purpose of this handbook Mục đích của cẩm nang

This handbook has been designed to provide a service to Crosby's customers by presenting reference data and technical recommendations based on many years of experience in sizing, selecting, testing, installing and operating pressure relief valves. Sufficient data is supplied to properly size and select Crosby pressure relief valves for specific applications. Terminology, standards, codes, basic design, sizing and selection are covered in the following chapters.

A note on CROSBY-SIZE Một lưu ý về chương trình CROSBY-SIZE

Crosby also offers the CROSBY-SIZE computer sizing program. Program control via pop-up windows, function keys and extensive on-line help makes it easy to understand and operate. It is assumed that the user has a basic understanding of relief valve sizing calculations and is responsible for correct determination of service conditions and the suitability of the program for a specific application. Both CROSBY-SIZE and this Engineering Handbook are useful tools in sizing pressure relief valves; should additional clarification be required, contact Crosby.
