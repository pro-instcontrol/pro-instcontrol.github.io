Fundamentals of Pressure Relief Valve Design

Crosby Valve Inc. · Technical Document TP-V300

Chapter 2 · 6 source pages · 6 figures · companion notes
Key terms. MAWP — maximum allowable working pressure · blowdown — the reclose ratio · huddling chamber — the control chamber · JLT — liquid trim · soft seat — elastomer seating.

A pressure relief valve is a safety device designed to protect a pressurized vessel or system during an overpressure event. An overpressure event is any condition that drives pressure beyond the specified design pressure or maximum allowable working pressure (MAWP). Being safety devices, relief valves are governed by many Codes and Standards.

Control / huddling chamber Buồng điều khiển và vòng miệng phun

The design of the control (huddling) chamber involves trade-offs: maximizing lift effort gives a long blowdown, while minimizing blowdown diminishes lift effort. Many valves are therefore fitted with an adjustable nozzle ring that varies the control-chamber geometry to meet a particular operating requirement.

Figure F2-2 is a simple sketch of the disc held closed by the spring. When system pressure reaches set pressure, the force of pressure over area A1 equals the spring force and the disc lifts to let fluid escape. As the disc lifts, the spring force increases, so system pressure must rise further (the allowable overpressure) to reach full lift.

Liquid trim designs Thiết kế trim lưu chất

For liquid service Crosby offers a patented liquid trim designated Style JLT-JOS or JLT-JBS (Figure F2-4), available in metal or soft-seated versions, giving stable non-chattering performance and high capacity at 10% overpressure.

Materials of construction Vật liệu chế tạo

Compatibility with the process fluid is achieved by careful material selection for strength, temperature and chemical resistance; seating surfaces must be finely finished (optically flat) for tight shut-off. Thermal expansion of mating parts is another design factor.

Back pressure considerations Xét đến áp suất ngược

Valves on clean, non-toxic, non-corrosive systems may vent to atmosphere; those on corrosive, toxic or valuable recoverable fluids vent into closed systems. Closed systems or long vent pipes can develop high back pressure that must be evaluated for its effect on performance and relieving capacity.

Seat leakage Rò rỉ tại đệm kín

Relief valves must hold tight over long periods. Allowable seat leakage limits are far more stringent than for other valves, achieved through part alignment, optically flat seating and suitable materials. Where system conditions permit, soft or elastomer seats (Figure F2-6) give additional tightness.

Pilot operated pressure relief valves Van vận hành bằng pilot

A pilot-operated PRV consists of a main valve with a piston- or diaphragm-operated disc and a pilot. Under normal conditions the pilot admits system pressure into the piston chamber; because the piston area exceeds the disc seat area, the disc is held closed. At set pressure the pilot shuts off supply to the piston chamber and vents it, opening the disc. Pilots allow operating pressure to reach within 5% of set pressure without increased seat leakage, and can be set to close with very short blowdown.

Required relieving capacity Lưu lượng xả cần thiết

The valve must relieve enough fluid that pressure never exceeds the specified overpressure. All credible overpressure sources (failed shut-off valve, control failure, fire, pump failure, runaway reaction, isolation, etc.) are evaluated and the worst-case combination sets the required capacity. Total rated relieving capacity of the selected valve(s) must exceed that requirement.

Summary: this chapter introduces the design considerations and governing Codes and Standards, emphasizing that a relief valve is a safety device that protects vessels and systems from catastrophic failure.
