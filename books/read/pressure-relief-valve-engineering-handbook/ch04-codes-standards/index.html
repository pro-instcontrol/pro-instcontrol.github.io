Codes and Standards — Summary

Crosby Valve Inc. · Technical Document TP-V300

Chapter 4 · 3 source pages · companion notes
Key terms in this chapter. API — American Petroleum Institute · ASME — American Society of Mechanical Engineers · BPVC — Boiler and Pressure Vessel Code · MSS-SP — Manufacturers Standardization Society · psig — pounds per square inch gauge.

This chapter contains a summary of the Codes and Standards applicable to the specification, sizing, selection, installation and testing of pressure relief valves. The listing is a pointer to the applicable documents, not a substitute for the source texts themselves.

American Petroleum Institute (API) Viện Dầu khí Hoa Kỳ (API)

ANSI/API RP 520 Part I, Sizing and Selection ANSI/API RP 520 Phần I — Định cỡ và Lựa chọn

This API design manual is widely used for sizing of relief valves on both liquid and gas filled vessels: (a) liquid vessels — paragraphs 5 and 6, and (b) gas filled vessels — Appendix D-3. This RP covers only vessels above 15 psig.

ANSI/API RP 520 Part II, Installation ANSI/API RP 520 Phần II — Lắp đặt

This includes: (a) recommended piping practices, (b) calculation formula for reactive force on valve (2.4), and (c) precautions on preinstallation handling and dirt.

ANSI/API RP 521, Guide for Pressure Relief and Depressuring Systems ANSI/API RP 521 — Hệ thống Xả áp và Hạ áp

An excellent document on everything from causes of overpressure through flare stacks.

ANSI/API RP 526, Flanged Steel Relief Valves ANSI/API RP 526 — Van xả áp thép mặt bích

Gives industry standards as to dimensions, pressure-temperature ratings, maximum set pressures, body materials.

ANSI/API RP 527, Seat Tightness of Pressure Relief Valves ANSI/API RP 527 — Độ kín đệm van xả áp

Permissible leakage rate of conventional and bellows valves and testing procedure.

API Guide for Inspection of Refinery Equipment, Chapter XVI — Pressure Relieving Devices API Hướng dẫn Kiểm tra, Chương XVI

Gives: (a) guide for inspection and record keeping, and (b) frequency of inspection, Paragraph 1602.03.

American Society of Mechanical Engineers (ASME) / ANSI Hội Kỹ sư Cơ khí Hoa Kỳ (ASME) / ANSI

ASME B31.1. Power Piping — Code 1995 Edition ASME B31.1 — Đường ống Công suất

Reference sections: Chapter II, Part 3, Paragraph 107.8 Safety and relief valves including general information, safety and relief valves on boiler external piping, safety relief valves on non boiler external piping, and non mandatory appendices on valve installations; and Chapter II, Part 6, Paragraph 122.6 — Pressure Relief Piping.

ASME/ANSI B16.5. Pipe flanges and flanged fittings ASME/ANSI B16.5 — Mặt bích ống

This standard provides allowable materials, pressure temperature limits and flange dimensions for standard ANSI flanges.

ASME/ANSI B16.34. Valves — Flanged, Threaded and Welding End ASME/ANSI B16.34 — Van mặt bích, ren và hàn

Standard covers pressure, temperature ratings, dimensions, tolerances, materials, nondestructive examination requirements, testing and marking for cast, forged and manufactured flanged, threaded and welding end valves. (End connection dimensions and tolerances are applicable only.)

ANSI B31.8. Gas Transmission and Distribution Systems ANSI B31.8 — Hệ thống Truyền tải Khí

Portions of this large document pertain to pressure relief and its limitations.

Manufacturers Standardization Society (MSS-SP) Hội Tiêu chuẩn hóa của ngành Van và Phụ kiện (MSS-SP)

SP-25. Standard marking system for valves, fittings, flanges and unions SP-25 — Hệ thống đánh dấu chuẩn

(Not applicable to pressure relief valves.) Refer to UG-129 of ASME Section VIII for marking information for pressure relief valves.

SP-55. Quality standards for steel castings SP-55 — Tiêu chuẩn chất lượng đúc thép

Quality standards for steel castings for valves, flanges and fittings and other piping components.

SP-61. Pressure testing of steel valves SP-61 — Thử áp suất van thép

(Not applicable to pressure relief valves.) Refer to API Recommended Practice 527 for commercial seat tightness tests.

Other Standards to be considered: See pages 4-2 and 4-3.
