Valve Sizing and Selection — U.S.C.S. Units

Crosby Valve Inc. · Technical Document TP-V300

Chapter 5 · 10 source pages · companion notes
Key terms. MAWP — maximum allowable working pressure · overpressure — above set pressure · back pressure: superimposed / built-up · effective discharge area · discharge coefficient K = 0.975 · K_b, K_sh, K_n, K_w, K_v — correction factors · cold differential test pressure (CDTP) · combination capacity factor.

This section of the Crosby Pressure Relief Valve Engineering Handbook is designed to assist the user in the sizing and selection of pressure relief valves when system parameters are expressed in U.S.C.S. units. The basic formulae and capacity correction factors reflect current state-of-the-art technology and are consistent with the requirements of ASME Section VIII and API Recommended Practice 520.

The sizing formulae calculate the required effective discharge area for a valve that will flow the required volume of system fluid at the anticipated relieving conditions; the valve size and style are then selected to have a nominal effective area equal to or greater than the calculated value. Effective areas for Crosby valves are listed on pages 7-30 and 7-31. Valve selection and sizing is the responsibility of the system engineer and the user; it must not be based on arbitrarily assumed conditions or incomplete information.

Crosby offers the CROSBY-SIZE computer program for sizing pressure relief valves (see page 1-1 or contact your local Crosby Representative).

Required Sizing Data Dữ liệu định cỡ cần thiết

The following is a suggested list of service conditions which must be provided in order to properly size and select a pressure relief valve.

Fluid Properties Tính chất chất lưu

  • Fluid and state
  • Molecular weight
  • Viscosity
  • Specific gravity — liquid referred to water, gas referred to air
  • Ratio of specific heats, k
  • Compressibility factor, Z

Operating Conditions Điều kiện vận hành

  • Operating pressure (psig maximum)
  • Operating temperature (°F maximum)
  • Maximum allowable working pressure, MAWP (psig)

Relieving Conditions Điều kiện xả

  • Required relieving capacity — gas or vapor (lb/hr), gas or vapor (scfm), liquid (gpm)
  • Set pressure (psig)
  • Allowable overpressure (%)
  • Superimposed back pressure (psig) — specify constant or variable
  • Built-up back pressure (psig)
  • Relieving temperature (°F)

Gas and Vapor Sizing — 10% Overpressure (lb/hr) Định cỡ khí và hơi — quá áp 10% (lb/hr)

This formula is used for gases and vapor (except steam) when required flow is expressed as a mass flow rate, pounds per hour. Correction factors account for the effects of back pressure, compressibility and subcritical flow.

(5.1)A = W √T ZC K P1 Kb √M
  • A — minimum required effective discharge area, in²
  • W — required relieving capacity, lb/hr
  • T — absolute temperature at the valve inlet, °R = °F + 460
  • Z — compressibility factor (Figure F7-1, page 7-2); use Z = 1.0 if unknown
  • C — coefficient from the ratio of specific heats (Table T7-7, page 7-26); use C = 315 if unknown
  • K — effective coefficient of discharge, K = 0.975
  • K_b — capacity correction factor for back pressure (Table T7-1 page 7-3 for conventional valves; Figure F7-2 page 7-5 for bellows/Series BP; pilot valves see page 7-4)
  • M — molecular weight of the gas or vapor (Table T7-7, page 7-26)
  • P1 — relieving pressure, psia = set pressure + overpressure + atmospheric

Example 1 — Atmospheric Back Pressure Ví dụ 1 — Áp suất ngược khí quyển

Fluid: Natural Gas; capacity: 5,900 lb/hr; set pressure: 210 psig; overpressure: 10%; relieving temp: 120°F; molecular weight: 19.0. With T = 580°R, P₁ = 245.7 psia, C = 344, K_b = 1.0 (atmospheric), A = 0.396 in². Select a 1-1/2G2-1/2 Style JOS-15 with Type J cap — the "G" orifice (0.503 in²) is the smallest that flows the required capacity. Standard materials are satisfactory (natural gas).

Example 2 — Superimposed Constant Back Pressure Ví dụ 2 — Áp suất ngược chép lên không đổi

Same as Example 1 but with a superimposed constant back pressure of 195 psig. Back-pressure percentage = (195 + 14.7)/(210 + 21 + 14.7) = 85.3%; interpolating Table T7-1 gives K_b = 0.76. Recomputed A = 0.520 in². Select a conventional Style JOS valve, 1-1/2H3 JOS-15 with Type J cap. The cold differential test pressure (CDTP) = set pressure − superimposed back pressure = 15 psig; under service the valve opens at 210 psig (= 15 + 195).

Example 3 — Set Pressure Below 30 psig Ví dụ 3 — Áp suất thiết lập dưới 30 psig

ASME Section VIII specifies a maximum allowable overpressure of 3 psi for set pressures below 30 psig. Fluid: air (UV stamp required); capacity: 500 lb/hr; set pressure: 20 psig; overpressure: 3 psi; T = 530°R; P₁ = 37.7 psia. A = 0.163 in². Select a 1"×1-1/2" Crosby Series 900 valve with a No. 7 (0.196 in²) orifice, Type D lifting lever — Model 972103M-D.

Example 4 — Variable Superimposed Back Pressure Ví dụ 4 — Áp suất ngược chép lên biến thiên

When a relief valve is exposed to variable back pressure, its set pressure is affected unless a balanced bellows or Series BP valve is selected. Fluid: air (UV stamp); capacity: 280 lb/hr; T = 600°R; P₁ = 78.5 psia; K_b from Table F7-2 for BP-Omni = 0.650. A = 0.072 in². Select a 3/4"×1" Series BP with a 0.074 in² orifice, Type D lever — Model BP51701M-D.

Gas and Vapor Sizing — 10% Overpressure (scfm) Định cỡ khí và hơi — quá áp 10% (scfm)

This formula is used for gases and vapor when required flow is expressed as a volumetric flow rate, scfm. Correction factors account for back pressure, compressibility and subcritical flow.

(5.2)A = SCFM √T G Z1.175 C K P1 Kb
  • A — minimum required effective discharge area, in²
  • SCFM — required relieving capacity, standard cubic feet per minute
  • T — absolute temperature at the valve inlet, °R = °F + 460
  • G — specific gravity of the gas or vapor (relative to air)
  • Z — compressibility factor (Figure F7-1); use Z = 1.0 if unknown
  • C — coefficient from the ratio of specific heats (Table T7-7); use C = 315 if unknown
  • K = 0.975; K_b — capacity correction factor for back pressure (as above)
  • P1 — relieving pressure, psia

Example 1 — Built-up Variable Back Pressure Ví dụ 1 — Áp suất ngược tạo ra biến thiên

Fluid: Ethylene Gas; capacity: 12,000 scfm; set pressure: 170 psig; overpressure: 10%; back pressure: 0–75 psig; inlet temp: 200°F; specific gravity: 0.968. Back pressure percentage = 75/170 = 44.1%; K_b = 0.899 (bellows style, Figure F7-2). Standard valve: A = 4.282 in² → select an "N" orifice (4.34 in²), Model 4N6 JBS-15, Type L cap. Pilot Style JPV: its performance is unaffected by back pressure, so K_b is not applicable (except for subcritical flow); A = 3.849 in² → select a 4N6 JPV-15.

Steam Sizing — 10% Overpressure (lb/hr) Định cỡ hơi nước — quá áp 10% (lb/hr)

This formula is based on the empirical Napier formula for steam flow. Correction factors account for superheat, back pressure and subcritical flow. An additional factor K_n is required by ASME when the relieving pressure (P₁) is above 1500 psia.

(5.3)A = W51.5 K P1 Ksh Kn Kb
  • A — minimum required effective discharge area, in²
  • W — required relieving capacity, lb/hr
  • K = 0.975
  • P1 — relieving pressure, psia
  • K_sh — superheat correction factor (Table T7-2, page 7-8); K_sh = 1.00 for saturated steam
  • K_n — dry saturated steam at set pressures above 1500 psia (Figure F7-4, page 7-6)
  • K_b — capacity correction factor for back pressure (as above)

Example 1 — Saturated Steam Ví dụ 1 — Hơi bão hòa

Capacity: 21,500 lb/hr; set pressure: 225 psig; overpressure: 10%; P₁ = 262.2 psia. A = 1.633 in² → select a "K" orifice (1.838 in²), Model 3K4 JOS-15 with Type C lifting lever. Standard materials are satisfactory.

Example 2 — Superheated Steam Ví dụ 2 — Hơi quá nhiệt

Capacity: 108,500 lb/hr; relieving temp: 750°F; set pressure: 532 psig; P₁ = 599.9 psia. Superheat correction K_sh = 0.844 (page 7-8). A = 4.268 in² → select an "N" orifice (4.34 in²), Model 4N6 JOS-46 with Type C lever and alloy steel spring.

Example 3 — Saturated Steam at Relieving Pressure > 1500 psig Ví dụ 3 — Hơi bão hòa tại áp suất xả > 1.500 psig

Capacity: 88,000 lb/hr; set pressure: 2,750 psig; P₁ = 3,039.7 psia. Capacity correction K_n = 1.155 (Figure F7-4). A = 0.499 in² → select a "G" orifice (0.503 in²), Model 2G3 JOS-76 with Type C lever and alloy steel spring.

Liquid Sizing — Spring Loaded Valves Định cỡ chất lỏng — van lò xo (Spring Loaded)

This formula applies to Styles JLT-JOS, JLT-JBS, Series 900 and Series BP, using capacities certified by the National Board of Boiler and Pressure Vessel Inspectors per ASME Section VIII. Sizing with this formulation is not permitted for overpressures less than 10%.

(5.4)A = GPM √G28.14 Kw Kv √ΔP
  • A — minimum required effective discharge area, in²
  • G — specific gravity of the liquid at flowing conditions
  • GPM — required relieving capacity, U.S. gallons per minute at flowing temperature
  • ΔP — differential pressure (psi) = set pressure + overpressure − back pressure
  • K_v — flow correction factor due to viscosity (page 7-7)
  • K_w — capacity correction factor for back pressure on bellows or Series BP valves (Figure F7-3, page 7-5)

Example 1 — Liquid, gpm Ví dụ 1 — Chất lỏng (gpm)

Fluid: Sodium Trisulfate; capacity: 125 gpm; set pressure: 100 psig; overpressure: 10%; built-up back pressure: 0–30 psig; temp: 60°F; G = 1.23. K_w = 0.866 (Figure F7-3), ΔP = 80 psi. A = 0.636 in² → select an "H" orifice (0.785 in²). Because built-up back pressure exceeds 10%, a bellows Style JBS is required; Model 1-1/2H3 JLT-JBS-15 with Type J cap.

Example 2 — Liquid, gpm Ví dụ 2 — Chất lỏng (gpm)

Fluid: Castor Oil; capacity: 100 gpm; set pressure: 210 psig; overpressure: 10%; constant back pressure: 35 psig; G = 0.96. K_w = 1.0, ΔP = 196 psi. A = 0.249 in² → select a Series 900 OMNI-TRIM valve with an "8" orifice (0.307 in²). Because the back pressure is constant a conventional Style JOS or Series 900 may be used; Model 981105M-A.

Liquid Sizing — Pilot Operated Valves (Style JPVM) Định cỡ chất lỏng — van vận hành pilot (Style JPVM)

Crosby Style JPVM pilot operated valves may be used on liquid service and provide 30% greater capacity than spring loaded valves with liquid trim, permitting a smaller valve. Viscosity should be no greater than 300 SSU (then K_v = 1.0 may be used). See page 7-25 for two-phase flow.
(5.5)A = GPM √G36.81 Kv √ΔP
  • A — minimum required effective discharge area, in²
  • G — specific gravity of the liquid at flowing conditions
  • GPM — required relieving capacity, U.S. gallons per minute at flowing temperature
  • ΔP — differential pressure (psi) = set pressure + overpressure − back pressure
  • K_v — flow correction factor due to viscosity (page 7-7)

Example 1 — Liquid GPM, Crosby Style JPVM Ví dụ 1 — Chất lỏng GPM, van Style JPVM

Fluid: Sodium Trisulfate; capacity: 125 GPM; set pressure: 100 psig; overpressure: 10%; built-up back pressure: 0–30 psig; G = 1.23; K_v = 1.0; ΔP = 80 psi. A = 0.421 in² → select a "G" orifice (0.503 in²), Model 1-1/2G3 Style JPVM-15.

Multiple Valve Sizing Định cỡ nhiều van

When multiple pressure relief valves are used, one valve shall be set at or below the MAWP and the remaining valve(s) may be set up to 5% over the MAWP. The total required area is calculated on an overpressure of 16% or 4 psi, whichever is greater. For fire exposure refer to liquid relief valve sizing under fire conditions (page 7-17).

Example 1 — Natural Gas Ví dụ 1 — Khí tự nhiên

MAWP: 210 psig; capacity: 5,900 lb/hr; set pressure: 210 psig; overpressure: 16%; T = 580°R; molecular weight: 19.0. P₁ = 258.3 psia. A = 0.376 in² → select two "E" orifice valves (0.196 in² each, total 0.392 in²): one set at MAWP = 210 psig and one at 105% MAWP = 220.5 psig. Model 1E2 JOS-15-J.

Example 2 — Air Ví dụ 2 — Khí

Capacity: 150,000 lb/hr; set pressure: 200 psig; overpressure: 16%; T = 610°R; molecular weight: 28.97; P₁ = 246.7 psia; C = 356. A = 8.038 in². Select two "N" orifice valves (4.340 in² each, total 8.680 in²). Model 4N6 JOS-15-J.

Combination Devices (Rupture Disc + Relief Valve) Thiết bị kết hợp (đĩa đứt + van xả áp)

The rated relieving capacity of a pressure relief valve in combination with a rupture disc is equal to the capacity of the relief valve multiplied by a combination capacity factor to account for flow losses attributed to the rupture disc. When a tested combination factor for the specific rupture disc and relief valve is not available, a factor of 0.9 may be used. Tested factors are compiled by the National Board in NB-18.

Example 1 — Gas/Vapor Mass Flow (lb/hr) Ví dụ 1 — Khí/hơi, lưu lượng khối lượng (lb/hr)

Fluid: Natural Gas; capacity: 7,400 lb/hr; set pressure: 210 psig; overpressure: 10%; T = 580°R; molecular weight: 19.0; P₁ = 245.7 psia; C = 344. A = 0.496 in². With a rupture disc combination factor of 0.9 the required area = 0.496/0.9 = 0.551 in² → an "H" orifice (0.785 in²) is needed, one size larger. If a specific rupture disc with a combination factor of 0.986 or higher (with Crosby valves) is used, a larger size may not be necessary (see NB-18, Section IV).
