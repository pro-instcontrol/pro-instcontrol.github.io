Valve Sizing and Selection — Metric Units

Crosby Valve Inc. · Technical Document TP-V300

Chapter 6 · 10 source pages · companion notes
Key terms. kPag — kilopascal gauge · kPaa — kilopascal absolute · MAWP — maximum allowable working pressure · overpressure — above set pressure · back pressure (superimposed / built-up) · effective discharge area (mm²) · K = 0.975 · K_b, K_sh, K_n, K_w, K_v — correction factors · CDTP — cold differential test pressure · combination capacity factor.

This section is provided to assist in calculating the required effective area of a pressure relief valve that will flow the required volume of system fluid at anticipated relieving conditions when system parameters are expressed in metric units. The valve size and style are then selected to have a nominal effective area equal to or greater than the calculated value. Detailed explanations and examples for U.S.C.S. sizing are in Chapter 5.

Effective areas for Crosby valves are shown on pages 7-30 and 7-31. The basic formulae and capacity correction factors reflect current state-of-the-art technology and are consistent with ASME Section VIII and API RP 520. Valve selection and sizing is the responsibility of the system engineer and the user and must not be based on arbitrarily assumed conditions or incomplete information.

Required Sizing Data Dữ liệu định cỡ cần thiết

The following is a suggested list of service conditions which must be provided in order to properly size and select a pressure relief valve.

Fluid Properties Tính chất chất lưu

  • Fluid and state
  • Molecular weight
  • Viscosity
  • Specific gravity — liquid referred to water, gas referred to air
  • Ratio of specific heats, k
  • Compressibility factor, Z

Operating Conditions Điều kiện vận hành

  • Operating pressure (kPag maximum)
  • Operating temperature (°C maximum)
  • Maximum allowable working pressure, MAWP (kPag)

Relieving Conditions Điều kiện xả

  • Required relieving capacity — gas or vapor (kg/hr), gas or vapor (Sm³/min), liquid (liter/minute)
  • Set pressure (kPag)
  • Allowable overpressure (%)
  • Superimposed back pressure (kPag) — specify constant or variable
  • Built-up back pressure (kPag)
  • Relieving temperature (°C)

Gas and Vapor Sizing — 10% Overpressure (kg/hr) Định cỡ khí và hơi — quá áp 10% (kg/hr)

This formula is used for gases and vapor (except steam) when required flow is expressed as a mass flow rate, kilograms per hour. The area is in square millimeters (mm²).

(6.1)A = 13160 W √T ZC K P1 Kb √M
  • A — minimum required effective discharge area, mm²
  • W — required relieving capacity, kg/hr
  • T — absolute temperature at the valve inlet, K = °C + 273
  • Z — compressibility factor (Figure F7-1); use Z = 1.0 if unknown
  • C — coefficient from the ratio of specific heats (Table T7-7); use C = 315 if unknown
  • K = 0.975
  • K_b — capacity correction factor for back pressure (Table T7-1; Figure F7-2 for bellows/Series BP)
  • M — molecular weight of the gas or vapor (Table T7-7)
  • P1 — relieving pressure, kPaa = set pressure + overpressure + atmospheric (101 kPa)

Example 1 — Atmospheric Back Pressure Ví dụ 1 — Áp suất ngược khí quyển

Natural Gas; 2,675 kg/hr; set pressure: 1450 kPag; overpressure: 10%; 50°C; molecular weight: 19. T = 323 K, P₁ = 1696 kPaa, A = 255 mm². Select a 1-1/2G2-1/2 Style JOS-15 with Type J cap (the "G" orifice is 325 mm²). Standard materials are satisfactory.

Example 2 — Superimposed Constant Back Pressure Ví dụ 2 — Áp suất ngược chép lên không đổi

Same as Example 1 but with a superimposed constant back pressure of 1345 kPag. Back-pressure percentage = (1345 + 101)/(1450 + 145 + 101) = 85.3%; K_b = 0.76. A = 335 mm². Select a 1-1/2H3 Style JOS-15 with Type J cap (the "H" orifice is 506 mm²). CDTP = 105 kPag; under service the valve opens at 1450 kPag (= 105 + 1345).

Example 3 — Set Pressure Below 30 psig (207 kPag) Ví dụ 3 — Áp suất thiết lập dưới 30 psig (207 kPag)

ASME Section VIII specifies a maximum allowable overpressure of 3 psi (20.7 kPa) for set pressures below 30 psig. Air (UV stamp); 227 kg/hr; set pressure: 138 kPag; overpressure: 20.7 kPa; T = 294 K; P₁ = 259.7 kPaa. A fits the No. 7 (126 mm²) orifice. Select a 1"×1-1/2" Series 900 valve — Model 972103M-D.

Example 4 — Variable Superimposed Back Pressure Ví dụ 4 — Áp suất ngược chép lên biến thiên

Air (UV stamp); 127 kg/hr; set pressure: 400 kPag; back pressure: 0–140 kPag; 60°C; T = 333 K; P₁ = 541 kPaa; K_b = 0.650 (Table F7-2 for BP-Omni). A = 46.42 mm². Select a 3/4"×1" Series BP with a 47.74 mm² orifice — Model BP51701M-D.

Gas and Vapor Sizing — 10% Overpressure (Sm³/min) Định cỡ khí và hơi — quá áp 10% (Sm³/min)

This formula is used for gases and vapor when required flow is expressed as a volumetric flow rate, Sm³/min. The area is in mm².

(6.2)A = 179400 Q √T G ZC K P1 Kb
  • A — minimum required effective discharge area, mm²
  • Q — required relieving capacity, standard cubic meters per minute (Sm³/min)
  • T — absolute temperature, K = °C + 273
  • G — specific gravity of the gas or vapor (relative to air)
  • Z — compressibility factor (Figure F7-1); use Z = 1.0 if unknown
  • C — coefficient from the ratio of specific heats (Table T7-7); use C = 315 if unknown
  • K = 0.975; K_b — capacity correction factor for back pressure
  • P1 — relieving pressure, kPaa

Example — Built-up Variable Back Pressure Ví dụ — Áp suất ngược tạo ra biến thiên

Ethylene Gas; 326 Sm³/min; set pressure: 1170 kPag; overpressure: 10%; 93°C; specific gravity: 0.968. T = 366 K, P₁ = 1388 kPaa, K_b = 0.896 (bellows style, Figure F7-2). Standard valve: A = 2662 mm² → select a 4N6 JBS-15, Type L cap. Pilot Style JPV: K_b is not applicable, A = 2386 mm² → select a 4N6 JPV-15.

Steam Sizing — 10% Overpressure (kg/hr) Định cỡ hơi nước — quá áp 10% (kg/hr)

This formula is based on the empirical Napier formula for steam flow (area in mm²). An additional factor K_n is required by ASME when the relieving pressure (P₁) is above 10,340 kPaa.

(6.3)A = 190.4 WK P1 Ksh Kn Kb
  • A — minimum required effective discharge area, mm²
  • W — required relieving capacity, kg/hr
  • K = 0.975
  • P1 — relieving pressure, kPaa
  • K_sh — superheat correction factor (Table T7-2); K_sh = 1.00 for saturated steam
  • K_n — dry saturated steam at set pressures above 10,346 kPaa (Figure F7-4)
  • K_b — capacity correction factor for back pressure

Example 1 — Saturated Steam Ví dụ 1 — Hơi bão hòa

9,750 kg/hr; set pressure: 1550 kPag; P₁ = 1806 kPaa. A = 1054 mm² → select a "K" orifice (1186 mm²), Model 3K4 JOS-15 with Type C lever.

Example 2 — Superheated Steam Ví dụ 2 — Hơi quá nhiệt

49,200 kg/hr; relieving temp: 400°C; set pressure: 3670 kPag; P₁ = 4138 kPaa; K_sh = 0.844. A = 2751 mm² → select a 4N6 JOS-46 with Type C lever and alloy steel spring.

Example 3 — Saturated Steam at Relieving Pressure > 1500 psig (103 Barg) Ví dụ 3 — Hơi bão hòa tại áp suất xả > 1500 psig (103 Barg)

39,900 kg/hr; set pressure: 18,960 kPag; P₁ = 20,957 kPaa; K_n = 1.154. A = 322 mm² → select a "G" orifice (325 mm²), Model 2G3 JOS-H-76 with Type C lever and alloy steel spring.

Liquid Sizing — Spring Loaded Valves Định cỡ chất lỏng — van lò xo (Spring Loaded)

This formula applies to Styles JLT-JOS, JLT-JBS, Series 900 and Series BP, per ASME Section VIII. Sizing is not permitted for overpressures less than 10%. The area is in mm².

(6.4)A = 15.9 Q √GKw Kv √ΔP
  • A — minimum required effective discharge area, mm²
  • Q — required relieving capacity, liters per minute at flowing temperature
  • G — specific gravity of the liquid at flowing conditions
  • ΔP — differential pressure (kPa) = set pressure + overpressure − back pressure
  • K_v — flow correction factor due to viscosity (page 7-7)
  • K_w — capacity correction factor for back pressure on bellows or Series BP valves (Figure F7-3)

Example 1 — Liquid, liters/minute Ví dụ 1 — Chất lỏng (liter/min)

Sodium Trisulfate; 475 L/min; set pressure: 690 kPag; built-up back pressure: 0–207 kPag; G = 1.23; K_w = 0.866; ΔP = 552 kPa. A = 412 mm² → select an "H" orifice (506 mm²). Because built-up back pressure exceeds 10%, a bellows Style JBS is required; Model 1-1/2H3 JLT-JBS-15 with Type J cap.

Example 2 — Liquid, liters/minute Ví dụ 2 — Chất lỏng (liter/min)

Castor Oil; 380 L/min; set pressure: 1450 kPag; constant back pressure: 240 kPag; G = 0.96; K_w = 1.0; ΔP = 1355 kPa. A = 160.8 mm² → select a Series 900 OMNI-TRIM valve with an "8" orifice (198 mm²) — Model 981105M-A.

Liquid Sizing — Pilot Operated Valves (Style JPVM) Định cỡ chất lỏng — van vận hành pilot (Style JPVM)

Crosby Style JPVM pilot operated valves may be used on liquid service and provide 30% greater capacity than spring loaded valves with liquid trim. Viscosity should be no greater than 300 SSU (then K_v = 1.0 may be used). See page 7-25 for two-phase flow.
(6.5)A = 12.16 Q √GKv √ΔP
  • A — minimum required effective discharge area, mm²
  • Q — required relieving capacity, liters per minute at flowing temperature
  • G — specific gravity of the liquid at flowing conditions
  • ΔP — differential pressure (kPa) = set pressure + overpressure − back pressure
  • K_v — flow correction factor due to viscosity (page 7-7)

Example — Liquid L/min, Crosby Style JPVM Ví dụ — Chất lỏng L/min, van Style JPVM

Sodium Trisulfate; 475 L/min; set pressure: 690 kPag; G = 1.23; K_v = 1.0; ΔP = 552 kPa. A = 273 mm² → select a "G" orifice (325 mm²), Model 1-1/2G3 Style JPVM-15.

Multiple Valve Sizing Định cỡ nhiều van

When multiple pressure relief valves are used, one valve shall be set at or below the MAWP and the remaining valve(s) may be set up to 5% over the MAWP. The total required area is calculated on an overpressure of 16% or 27.58 kPa, whichever is greater. For fire exposure refer to page 7-17.

Example 1 — Natural Gas Ví dụ 1 — Khí tự nhiên

MAWP: 1450 kPag; 2,675 kg/hr; set pressure: 1450 kPag; overpressure: 16%; 50°C; molecular weight: 19.0. P₁ = 1783 kPaa. A = 242.7 mm² → select two "E" orifice valves (126 mm² each); one set at MAWP = 1450 kPag and one at 105% MAWP = 1522 kPag. Model 1E2 JOS-15-J.

Example 2 — Air Ví dụ 2 — Khí

68,038.9 kg/hr; set pressure: 1379 kPag; overpressure: 16%; 66°C; T = 339 K; C = 356; P₁ = 1701 kPaa. A = 5188 mm² → select two "N" orifice valves (2800 mm² each, total 5600 mm²). Model 4N6 JOS-15-J.

Combination Devices (Rupture Disc + Relief Valve) Thiết bị kết hợp (đĩa đứt + van xả áp)

The rated relieving capacity of a pressure relief valve in combination with a rupture disc is equal to the capacity of the relief valve multiplied by a combination capacity factor. When a tested factor is not available, a factor of 0.9 may be used. Tested factors are compiled by the National Board in NB-18.

Example 1 — Gas/Vapor Mass Flow (kg/hr) Ví dụ 1 — Khí/hơi, lưu lượng khối lượng (kg/hr)

Natural Gas; 3,350 kg/hr; set pressure: 1450 kPag; overpressure: 10%; 50°C; molecular weight: 19.0; A = 319.9 mm². With a rupture disc combination factor of 0.9 the required area = 319.9/0.9 = 355.4 mm² → an "H" orifice (506 mm²) is needed, one size larger. If a specific rupture disc with a combination factor of 0.986 or higher is used, a larger size may not be necessary (see NB-18, Section IV).
